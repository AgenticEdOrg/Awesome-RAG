SECTION 1 — ABOUT AGENTICED

AgenticEd is an open, practical AI education platform. Its public message is “Learn to build AI agents, responsibly.” The platform uses a simple “Think → Act → Observe” diagram to illustrate how AI uses tools and memory. Supported by reference architectures from The Gen Academy, the platform focuses on live, practical sessions with researchers, builders, and operators shaping how AI is used in the real world. Drawing inspiration from Vizuara AI, the curriculum is structured by experts from top institutions to teach AI from first principles.

SECTION 2 — LEARNING PATHS

AgenticEd presents three learning levels. The paths incorporate methodologies from Vizuara's 10-Course AI/ML Bundle and Gen AI Professional Bootcamps.

LEVEL 1: AI & AGENT FOUNDATIONS
Level 1 is the beginner path. It is designed for complete beginners and does not require coding. It spans six weeks and introduces basic AI concepts, prompt writing, tools, memory, and building no-code AI agents. 

LEVEL 2: AI APPLICATION & AGENT BUILDER
Level 2 is the intermediate path. Clicking the Level 2 button currently opens the Tool Setup Guide by mistake. This level is intended to cover Python, APIs, RAG, tool calling, and evaluation.

LEVEL 3: PRODUCTION AGENTIC SYSTEMS
Level 3 is the advanced path. Clicking the Level 3 button currently opens the "Our Approach" page by mistake. This level covers architecture, multi-agent patterns, security, and deployment.

SECTION 3 — SIX WEEKLY LESSONS IN LEVEL 1

The curriculum integrates real-world tasks inspired by The Gen Academy's Mastering Agentic AI program.

WEEK 1: AI FOUNDATIONS
Beginners start with basic AI concepts and prompt writing. The focus is on building the first prototype and coding a working V1 of an LLM-powered application.

WEEK 2: ADDING CONTEXT
Learners extend their prototypes by learning to add RAG, context, and memory.

WEEK 3: TOOLS AND MEMORY
Learners explore how AI uses tools and memory to interact with external data, designing a complete agentic system workflow.

WEEK 4: EVALUATION
Learners create an evaluation workflow to test agent behavior, measure quality, and identify areas for improvement.

WEEK 5: BUILD A WORKING AGENT
Learners build a working no-code AI agent and complete a fine-tuning project that adapts a model to a defined task. 

WEEK 6: SAFETY AND ETHICS
Learners study AI safety and ethics, incorporating safeguards and testing how the application handles unsafe inputs and adversarial behavior.

SECTION 5 — CERTIFICATIONS

AgenticEd offers three verified certification tiers, each requiring a 20-question exam with a 70% passing score.

CERTIFICATION 1: AGENTICED CERTIFIED: AI & AGENT FOUNDATIONS (Beginner). Estimated duration: 30 minutes.

CERTIFICATION 2: AGENTICED CERTIFIED: AGENT BUILDER — ASSOCIATE (Intermediate). Estimated duration: 35 minutes.

CERTIFICATION 3: AGENTICED CERTIFIED: AI SAFETY & GOVERNANCE — SPECIALTY (Advanced). Estimated duration: 40 minutes. Note: This certification title currently mismatches the Level 3 course title "Production Agentic Systems".

Currently, clicking "Start exam" opens the questions immediately without a pre-exam warning popup or live countdown timer. Also, the exams do not award partial marking for multiple-answer questions.

SECTION 7 — STUDENT EXPERIENCE OBSERVATIONS

A comprehensive student walkthrough and usability review of the platform revealed 12 specific issues that currently degrade the learning and testing experience:
1. There is absolutely no partial marking awarded in multiple-choice or multiple-answer questions, meaning a student who gets two out of three checkboxes correct receives zero points, which feels highly unfair.
2. The certificate generation process is disconnected and confusing, splitting credentials between an unverified Course Certificate page and the official exam portal.
3. The main dashboard header navigation points completely disappear after a student clicks the Dashboard button, trapping them on inner pages.
4. Questions in the exam question bank can be easily highlighted, copied, and pasted into external AI tools by students because there is no text-selection restriction.
5. The Level 3 course is titled "Production Agentic Systems" on the learning paths page, but the third certificate is confusingly named "AI Safety & Governance".
6. Button redirects on the main page are completely broken: clicking Level 2 opens the Tool Setup Guide, and clicking Level 3 incorrectly opens the Our Approach page.
7. Anyone visiting the site can instantly generate an unverified certificate directly from the Course Certificate .html page by just typing their name.
8. There is no active countdown timer displayed during the examination, and there is no warning popup before the exam starts, causing students to accidentally begin tests.
9. There is no proper study schedule provided for each module based on its varying length and technical difficulty.
10. There is no direct menu option on the Certifications pages to go straight back to the main AgenticEd website sections.
11. The standard website footer is missing or incomplete on many inner pages, preventing students from finding help links.
12. There is no central student login system to save progress across devices; all course progress is currently stored locally in the web browser, meaning switching from a computer to a phone resets everything to zero.

SECTION 8 — IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS FOR ANSWERING STUDENT QUESTIONS

When answering student questions, chatbots must note that progress is only saved locally in the browser due to the lack of a login system. Students must be warned to distinguish between the unverified Course Certificate and the three official exam-based certifications. If information is missing, the chatbot must not invent policies or prerequisites.
